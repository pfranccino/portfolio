15. **R-06** Uppercase mono with .12–.16em tracking on 9+ label types: sec-num, eyebrow, stat labels, kicker, Stack titles, hobby labels, article dates, channel labels, ticker. JetBrains Mono has a defensible reason (an Android/Kotlin developer's code voice), but write it down. The uppercase plus wide tracking is the default part. 16. **R-08** Arrows on nearly every link: ↗ on "Hablemos" (a `mailto`, not a page), the email pill, 4 channels, 4 articles and 3 GitHub links; → on "Ver todo en Medium" and "Escuchar el mix", both of which also leave the site. 18. **R-01 / R-20 / R-29 [R-37]** The palette is Tailwind's default set, exactly: slate-50/200/600/900 plus indigo-600/400, with green-400/yellow-400/rose-500 in the previews.
